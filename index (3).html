04 — How It Works

The Zayn Studio Custom Process

Your vision becomes reality through our four-step collaborative journey. From conceptualisation to delivery.

1

Conceptualise

Share your idea, dimensions, reference images or mood board. We discuss materials, finishes, colour palettes and timelines.

2

Design & Plan

Our studio team creates detailed renderings. We refine proportions, finalise materials, and prepare precision specifications.

3

Craft & Create

Handmade in our Coimbatore atelier. Laser-cutting, layering, finishing and hand-inspection ensure every piece is flawless.

4

Deliver

Delicately packaged and shipped. We provide installation guidance and lifetime support for any piece from Zayn Studio.

Questions?

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about our process, materials, and custom orders.

What materials do you use? +
We work with premium laser-cut acrylics, engineered MDF, brass hardware, linen, travertine, and carefully selected finishing materials. Every material is chosen for both durability and aesthetic contribution.
How long does a custom order take? +
Custom pieces typically take 3-4 weeks from final approval to delivery. Rush timelines are available for an additional fee. We'll provide a precise timeline during the conceptualisation phase.
Do you ship internationally? +
Currently, we ship across India with careful packaging and tracking. International orders are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact us directly to discuss your location.
What's your refund policy? +
Since all pieces are custom-made, we work to perfect your design before production. We stand behind our work with a quality guarantee and offer replacements for any manufacturing issues.
